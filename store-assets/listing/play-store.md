**Full description** (1733/4000):
```
PRASH is the tenant app for Parul University food court outlets. See everything you owe, pay it in a few taps and keep every receipt in one place.

PAY WITHOUT THE GUESSWORK
• One Pay screen lists everything due, in the order it must be paid: security deposit, container charges, extra charges, then monthly rent.
• Review what you are paying, then slide to pay. The app confirms with the payment gateway before showing success, so you never wonder whether it went through.
• If a payment is still being confirmed, PRASH tells you to wait instead of letting you pay twice.
• Pay by UPI, cards or net banking through Easebuzz.

RENT, CHARGES AND DEPOSIT
• Monthly rent with due dates, penalties and status at a glance.
• Extra charges and container charges, each with its own invoice and receipt.
• Security deposit with a clear invoice, payment and receipt.
• Download or share PDF invoices and receipts any time.

YOUR SHOP, ORGANISED
• Rental contracts and FSSAI licence in one place, with Active, Expiring and Expired labels.
• Switch between outlets from the home screen.

STAY INFORMED
• Alerts for new bills, due dates, payments received and failed payments, all in one list.

SIGN IN YOUR WAY
• Email and password, or Continue with Google.
• Forgot your password? A 4-digit code to your email, with automatic fill from your clipboard.
• Stay signed in for 90 days of activity. See where you are signed in, and sign out any time.

MODERN AND CONSIDERATE
• Clean new design with smooth navigation.
• Festival touches on the logo during major Indian festivals.
• Help and support one tap away: call or email, with support hours shown.

PRASH is for tenants of Parul University outlets. Your property manager creates your account.
```
